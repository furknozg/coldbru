FILTER DSL (version 0.1)
The DSL looks like JavaScript but is not JavaScript. An expression is a boolean condition over customer fields.

Operators
- Comparison: === (equal), !== (not equal), >, >=, <, <=. No coercion: a number field takes a number, a text field takes a quoted string.
- Membership: field in ["a", "b"]. The list is non-empty, literals only, at most 100 items.
- Logic: && (and), || (or), ! (not). Precedence from tight to loose: parentheses, !, comparisons and in, &&, ||. ! binds tighter than a comparison, so negate a comparison with parentheses: !(a === 1).
- Reserved and rejected: ~=, ==, !=, comparing two fields, a literal on the left.

Literals (always on the right-hand side)
- Text: "İstanbul" or 'İstanbul'. Escapes are only \\ \" \'. No line breaks inside text.
- Number: -?[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)? with no exponent, no leading +, no underscores. Int fields take whole numbers, money (Decimal) fields at most two decimals.
- Boolean: true, false. There is no null.
- Time: ago("30d") or ago("12h") is a point in time relative to now: a positive whole number plus d (days) or h (hours). date("2026-01-31") is an absolute date. A Date or DateTime field accepts date() with every operator; ago() only with >, >=, <, <=.

Relation predicates
- relation.some(x => condition): at least one related record satisfies the condition.
- relation.none(x => condition): no related record satisfies it.
- relation.every(x => condition): every related record satisfies it (true when there are none).
- x is a variable name you choose (one identifier, not a reserved word, not reused in a nested predicate). Every field of the related record is written with it: x.fieldName. Conditions inside one lambda must hold for the same record. Customer fields cannot be used inside a lambda body.

Reserved words: some, none, every, in, true, false, ago, date.

Size limits: at most 4096 bytes, 8 levels of nesting, 100 nodes.

Error codes you may see in tool results
- SYNTAX_ERROR: the text does not follow the grammar.
- UNSUPPORTED_OPERATOR: ~=, ==, != or a field-to-field comparison.
- UNSUPPORTED_EXPRESSION: a call or construct outside the DSL.
- INVALID_RELATION_SCOPE: a customer field inside a lambda, or a lambda variable reused.
- COMPLEXITY_LIMIT: the filter is too long, too deep or has too many items.
- UNKNOWN_FIELD, UNKNOWN_RELATION: a name that is not in the catalog; suggestions list close names.
- UNKNOWN_VALUE: a value outside a closed list or an unknown city; suggestions list valid values.
- TYPE_MISMATCH: the operator or literal does not fit the field type.
- EMPTY_AUDIENCE: the filter is valid but selects no reachable customer; loosen the conditions.
